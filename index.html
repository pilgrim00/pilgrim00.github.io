Jiaxiang Chen

Ph.D. student, Computer Science, Fudan University · Shanghai, China · 23110240003@m.fudan.edu.cn

I am a Ph.D. student at Fudan University. My research focuses on experience-guided structured reasoning for intelligent agents. I study how experience can be represented, reused, and refined to improve reasoning quality and decision robustness, spanning large language model reasoning, agent systems, and learning-based decision environments.

Experience

I began my research with generative modeling, working on GAN-based image synthesis and pose-guided generation, which provided a solid foundation in modeling, experimentation, and evaluation. Through internships at Tencent and Microsoft, I worked on generation quality improvement, data optimization, and prompt-based learning in real-world systems. I am currently a co-founder of AI2Apps, where I design and build deployable LLM-based agent systems, while my academic research centers on experience-guided reasoning and agent decision-making.

Research Projects

Experience-Guided Structured Reasoning for Agents

This line of work studies how experience extracted from successful reasoning or decision trajectories can be structured into reusable guidance, enabling more robust and controllable reasoning in intelligent agents.

LLM Reasoning Structured Inference Experience

Financial Agent and Decision Learning

I study learnable optimization and decision-making task environments in financial settings, emphasizing strategy learning, adaptation, and evolutionary dynamics rather than fixed or handcrafted trading agents.

Decision Learning Multi-Agent Finance

Agentic Systems and Deployment

This direction focuses on agentic system design and deployment, bridging research ideas with practical systems, including autonomous agent frameworks and end-to-end deployment pipelines.
